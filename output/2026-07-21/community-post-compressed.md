📝 SUMMARY

This week's coaching call featured project demos and strategic feedback, centering on Alireza's AgentTask launch. Hosted by Paul Miller and Patrick Chouinard, the session emphasized showing workflows rather than features, and identifying your ideal customer profile before building marketing. Members highlighted the shift to fully AI-generated code rather than manual writing.

💡 KEY INSIGHTS

Differentiate and demo workflows, not features or price. Patrick warned incumbents can undercut pricing overnight, so show complete 2-3 step user journeys rather than touring features.

Founders often skip the "wow factor." Details obvious to creators are frequently the most marketable to new users.

Use your own product to market itself. Ali was advised to use AgentTask to build his ICP strategy and communication methodology.

Identify your ICP before pitching. Research the ideal customer profile first, then tailor messaging to specific pain points.

Record short single-feature clips. Post focused demos to X or the forum for targeted feedback rather than lengthy walkthroughs.

AI "slop" is a moving target. Today's high-quality output becomes tomorrow's slop as models retrain on it, requiring continuous skill updates.

Teach skills by example with verification. Feed the AI wrong versus right outputs, iterating toward 90% first-pass quality. Use a separate verifier skill that evaluates design output against criteria rather than hoping the generative step self-corrects.

Observe before training. Watch clients work to identify pain points, then have Claude build a plan rather than delivering generic step-by-step courses.

Highly regulated fields suit AI document analysis. Legal and notarial work is text-based and procedurally defined, making augmentation straightforward when RAG resources are provided.

Idle-screen animations reduce UX friction. A looping 10-second animation showing a persona using the product guides users who don't know what to do.
